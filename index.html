About

I am currently a Class 11 student, actively building skills in technology, design, and problem-solving. My work focuses on both software and hardware—from developing websites and applications to experimenting with electronic systems and physical prototypes.

I have worked on multiple projects including web development, Python-based applications, and introductory machine learning models. Alongside this, I explore hardware by building functional devices and understanding how systems operate beyond the screen.

I am a thinker and a creative individual who enjoys analyzing ideas and turning them into practical solutions. My interests extend into automotive engineering—I am a car enthusiast with a strong curiosity about performance and design, along with a deep interest in Formula 1. I also appreciate precision and engineering through premium watches, reflecting my attention to detail.

I was born in Indore (Madhya Pradesh) and have lived in multiple cities including Tezpur and Guwahati, and I am currently based in Kolkata. Having studied in 6 different schools, I have experienced diverse environments and perspectives. Coming from a middle-class background, I understand real-world needs—especially those of students and everyday users—which helps me build more practical and relevant solutions.

I am comfortable communicating in multiple languages including Hindi, English, and Bengali, with a basic understanding of French. This multilingual exposure helps me connect with different audiences and think from broader perspectives.

I approach everything with a builder mindset—focusing on creating real systems, improving through execution, and thinking long-term. My goal is to grow into advanced technologies and eventually build scalable products and impactful businesses.

Vision

My vision is to build a technology ecosystem that seamlessly integrates software and hardware into unified, scalable systems. I aim to begin by creating high-impact digital products—websites, applications, and platforms—that solve real-world problems and deliver value efficiently.

As these products evolve, the focus expands toward platform-level thinking: connecting individual solutions into larger systems, optimizing performance, and designing architectures that scale across users, use cases, and industries. This stage emphasizes efficiency, usability, and long-term sustainability.

The next step is advancing into hardware innovation—developing devices that tightly integrate with software to create powerful, reliable, and user-centric experiences. This includes exploring domains such as consumer electronics and intelligent systems.

Ultimately, the objective is to build a global technology company that continuously innovates across software and hardware, delivering products that impact millions and shape the future of technology.

Direction: Apps → Platforms → Systems → Devices → Global Tech Company
